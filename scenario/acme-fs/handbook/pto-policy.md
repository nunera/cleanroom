# Paid time off

- Full-time employees accrue 6.67 hours of PTO per pay period (20 days a year).
- Up to 40 unused hours roll over into the next calendar year.
- Request PTO in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead; your manager approves it.
- Company holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day,
  Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

Questions go to hr@acme-logistics.example.
